Fissco – Five Stars Seafoods JSC

Committed to sustainability and global quality. We are one of the largest companies in Vietnam specializing in squid and cuttlefish sushi production, certified by HACCP and BRC, and trusted in demanding markets such as Korea, Japan, China, and the EU.


About Us

Founded in 1988, Quang Ninh Aquatic Products Export JSC No.2 is located in northern Vietnam, operating a factory of more than 4,000 m² with over 200 skilled workers.


In 2010, Five Stars Seafoods JSC (Fissco) was established in central Vietnam, with a factory area of about 4,000 m² and nearly 400 skilled workers. Additionally, we operate an international processing facility of 1,000 m² with 100 workers.


With more than 20 years of squid and cuttlefish sushi production under HACCP and BRC certification, our products have earned the trust of customers and established a strong foothold in fastidious markets worldwide.
