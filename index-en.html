Fissco – Five Stars Seafoods JSC

Committed to sustainability and global quality. We are a leading company in Vietnam specializing in squid and cuttlefish sushi production, certified by HACCP and BRC, and trusted in demanding markets such as Korea, Japan, China, and the EU.


About Us

In 2010, Five Stars Seafoods JSC (Fissco) was established in central Vietnam, operating a main factory area of about 4,000 m² with nearly 400 skilled workers. Additionally, we possess an international processing facility of over 1,000 m² powered by 100 dedicated employees.


With more than 20 years of experience in producing high-quality Squid and Cuttlefish sushi under strict HACCP and BRC standards, our products have consistently won the absolute trust of customers and secured a solid foothold in highly fastidious global markets.

Production Facilities

Seafood Processing Factory


Our processing facilities are fully integrated with modern equipment and advanced production technology, ensuring strict adherence to global food safety and quality control guidelines. Products
